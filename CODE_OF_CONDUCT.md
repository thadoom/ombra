# Code of conduct

Ombra's issues and discussions are for people who want their tracking to work better. Keep them friendly and useful.

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. Everyone started as a beginner.
- **No harassment**, insults, slurs or sexual content, in posts, links or attachments.
- **No spam**, ads, or links to repackaged or modified builds of Ombra.
- **Stay on topic.** Questions go to [Discussions](https://github.com/thadoom/ombra/discussions), bugs and proposals to [Issues](https://github.com/thadoom/ombra/issues/new/choose).
- **Protect your privacy.** Before you post a screenshot or a diagnostics file, check it for your name, your Windows user folder or anything else you'd rather keep private.

## If something goes wrong

Use **Report content** in the ⋯ menu of a post to flag it. The maintainer may edit or hide posts, lock threads and block accounts that break these rules.
