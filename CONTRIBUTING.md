# Contributing to Ombra

Ombra is free to use but closed source: there is no code to send pull requests to. You can still shape it, and most of what Ombra does today started as someone's idea.

## What you can propose

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| A new feature or extension: a new output, a game integration, a new tracker type, a calibration or drift idea | [Feature / extension proposal](https://github.com/thadoom/ombra/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| A new language, or a better translation | [Translation](https://github.com/thadoom/ombra/issues/new?template=translation.yml) |
| Something that doesn't work | [Bug report](https://github.com/thadoom/ombra/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| A question, or an idea you want to discuss first | [Discussions](https://github.com/thadoom/ombra/discussions) |

## A good proposal

- Start from the problem: the game, the pose or the moment where tracking falls short.
- Say what you'd like Ombra to do, and how you would notice that it works.
- Recordings help a lot: a short video, or the diagnostics file (Settings → **Export diagnostics**).
- For a new device or protocol, link its public documentation.

## What happens next

1. Proposals are read and labelled (`accepted`, `needs info`, `later`, `not planned`).
2. Accepted proposals are built into Ombra and credited in the release notes, unless you ask not to be named.
3. Beta builds are sometimes offered in the issue for testing before a release.

By sending a proposal, a translation or any other material you agree to the terms in section 3 of the [LICENSE](LICENSE): you have the right to share it, and Ombra may use it.

## Bug reports

Include your Ombra version (bottom left of the window), dongles, tracker firmware, headset and the diagnostics file. Steps to reproduce make a fix much faster.

## Be kind

Ombra is maintained in spare time. Be constructive and respectful with everyone.
