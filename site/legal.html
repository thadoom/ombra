Privacy

Ombra has no account, no ads and no analytics. It does not send anything about you, your body or your play to anyone.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, your body measurements, the calibration and each tracker's settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ombra. A diagnostics report is only made when you press Export diagnostics, and it stays on your PC until you decide to share it.

When Ombra goes online

  1. To check for a new version: it asks GitHub for the latest release. You can turn this off in Settings.
  2. For firmware: it asks Shiftall's server which firmware is the newest, and downloads the files when you update a tracker or a dongle.
  3. To send your body to VRChat or a VTuber app over OSC or VMC: only when you turn it on, and only to the address you set (your own PC by default).

Notifications on the wrist

When you turn on PC notifications for the wrist panel, Windows lets Ombra read the notifications your apps show. Ombra only shows them on your wrist for a few seconds: it does not save them and does not send them anywhere. You can take the permission back in Windows Settings, Privacy & security, Notifications.
